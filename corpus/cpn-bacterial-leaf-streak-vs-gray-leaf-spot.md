# Bacterial Leaf Streak vs. Gray Leaf Spot

Both diseases produce elongated lesions between the leaf veins, but they differ in appearance
and in where they show up in the canopy.

## Bacterial leaf streak (BLS)

- Lesions are tan to brown with **wavy margins**.
- Appears in the mid- to upper canopy.
- Frequently shows up following storm events.

## Gray leaf spot (GLS)

- Lesions are **rectangular with straight edges**.
- Appears first in the lower canopy.
- Moves progressively upward through the season.

## Field diagnostic technique

Hold the leaf up to the light. BLS lesions often show bright yellow halos, unlike GLS.

## Why the distinction matters

Fungicides work against GLS but not against BLS, which is caused by a bacterium rather than a
fungus. Misidentifying BLS as GLS leads to an ineffective fungicide application and to missed
prevention opportunities for future seasons.
